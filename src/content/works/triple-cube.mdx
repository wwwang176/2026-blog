<Prose title="Player vs. Player">

You play against a pre-recorded run of someone else playing Triple Cube. The
system records how fast your fingers place cubes on the map.

</Prose>

<Prose title="Pre-recorded and A.I.">

When you finish a round, the system records your finger speed as you place
cubes on the map. When another player is paired with you, the A.I. plays
against them on your behalf, driven by that recorded speed.

</Prose>
