<Prose title="Built from scratch">

Every low-poly model in the game — the cars, the road furniture, the
surrounding scenery — was modelled by hand in SketchUp rather than pulled
from an asset library.

</Prose>

<Prose title="Research">

Textures on distant surfaces turned to mush as the camera pulled away. Working
out why, and how to keep them sharp at range, became a piece of research in
its own right and eventually a write-up of its own.

</Prose>
